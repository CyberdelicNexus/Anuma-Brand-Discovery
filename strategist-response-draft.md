**Why does the brand need to change now?**  
The research has earned credibility, but the way people discover, understand and access the work has not caught up. The brand needs to make the value clear and give people a practical way in.

## 02 / Core truth

**Why was aNUma created?**  
To explore how immersive technology can create new experiences of embodiment, connection and meaning, then make that research something people can actually experience.

**What has aNUma learned through experience that others may not know yet?**  
The container matters as much as the experience. Preparation, agency, facilitation and integration shape what people can take from it.

**What must never be lost as aNUma evolves?**  
Epistemic humility, participant agency, scientific rigour, the numadelic aesthetic and the relational heart of the work.

**What would be missing if aNUma no longer existed?**  
A rare bridge between immersive design, contemplative practice and serious research.

## 03 / Ecosystem

**What role should aNUma play?**  
aNUma should package, deliver and sustain numadelic practices for participants and practitioners.

**What function should Numadelic Labs serve?**  
Numadelic Labs should research, improve and safeguard the field, while extending access through public-benefit work.

**How should people understand their relationship?**  
Distinct initiatives, visibly connected.

**Shared DNA**  
The numadelic method, values, aesthetic, community and commitment to evidence.

**Necessary difference**  
Governance, claims, revenue, product delivery and public-benefit responsibilities.

**Where does the current ecosystem create confusion?**  
It is not always clear who owns the product, who makes the scientific claims, where revenue goes or which name the public should trust.

## 04 / Product system

**How should the solo pathway work, feel and create value?**  
It should be a prepared journey, not a content feed: clear intention, readiness, consent, the experience, reflection and a next step.

**How should the group pathway support facilitators, clinics and labs?**  
Give them training, session tools, trusted experiences, participant safeguards, evidence and a way to offer recurring practices or cohorts.

**Which pricing logic feels most aligned?**  
We need to test the model.

**What should experience designers be able to create, control or publish?**  
They should build experiences through a no-code studio, test them in VR and submit them for review before publication.

**How should designers gain value, and how might aNUma share in it?**  
Start with paid training and creator tools. Add review services and revenue share only when there is a real audience and distribution channel.

**What role should the companion app play?**  
It should hold the before and after: preparation, consent, reflection, practice history, community and access to facilitators.

**Four companion functions**  
Preparation prompts before VR; Safety and consent check-ins; Insight capture after the session; Integration practice tracker.

**What should the community layer make possible?**  
Help people practise together, find trusted guidance, join cohorts and continue relationships beyond one experience.

**What becomes possible when the full ecosystem connects?**  
Someone can discover the work, enter safely, continue practising, find guidance and eventually contribute back to the field.

**How visibly should the three layers relate?**  
One ecosystem with named tiers.

**Three messages for the first 30 seconds**  
What aNUma is; Who it is for; How to take the next step.

## 05 / People

**Who is the most important audience for the next chapter?**  
Contemplatively curious people already looking for a meaningful embodied practice, especially when a trusted teacher or facilitator can introduce them.

**What are they already looking for?**  
A way to feel and practise what they may already understand intellectually, without dogma or empty wellness claims.

**What barriers might stop them?**  
Headset access, technical setup, unclear benefits, an unfamiliar category, safety concerns and no obvious place to begin.

**What assumption might they make?**  
That it is an abstract research demo, a VR art project or a psychedelic simulation rather than a real practice.

**Participants need**  
A clear promise, safe entry, meaningful guidance and a way to continue.

**Partners join because**  
It adds a distinctive embodied tool to work they already do with their communities.

**Funders trust**  
Published evidence, credible partners, participant stories, responsible claims and signs of real adoption.

**Critics deserve an answer to**  
Why VR is needed, how risks are handled and whether the practice creates dependency on technology.

## 06 / Story

**What practical problem do people face?**  
They can understand ideas about connection or presence without having a reliable way to experience and practise them.

**How does that make them feel?**  
Interested but disconnected; informed but not changed.

**What can aNUma say that proves it understands?**  
Knowing something is not the same as feeling it or living it.

**What makes aNUma a credible guide?**  
Years of immersive design, peer-reviewed research, participant experience and a team that understands both contemplative practice and technology.

**What simple path do we invite people to take?**  
Choose a practice. Prepare with care. Enter the experience. Reflect. Continue alone or with others.

**What becomes possible when it works?**  
An unfamiliar experience becomes a practice they can understand, return to and integrate into life.

## 07 / Character

**Five defining traits**  
Humble; Rigorous; Warm; Embodied; Inventive.

**Five traits to avoid**  
Dogmatic; Clinical; Elitist; Vague; Overpromising.

**We are**  
Rigorous, imaginative and human.

**But we are not**  
Clinical, mystical for effect or certain about what another person should experience.

**Vision**  
Technology that protects attention and supports deeper ways of relating.

**Anti-vision**  
Technology designed for extraction, passive consumption or dependency.

**Allies**  
Contemplative teachers, researchers, artists, facilitators, care partners and thoughtful technologists.

**Enemies**  
Attention extraction, empty spectacle, unsupported claims and endless invention without delivery.

**What should people trust aNUma to do?**  
Guide them into an unfamiliar experience with care, clarity and room to make their own meaning.

## 08 / Archetype

**Natural contribution — choose three**  
Create intimate, beautiful connection; Open new paths to self-discovery; Invent original worlds and tools.

**Credible method — choose two**  
Invite people beyond familiar boundaries; Deepen felt connection with self and others.

**Shadow risks — choose two**  
Promising transformation we cannot responsibly guarantee; Remaining in endless invention instead of shipping.

## 10 / Proof

**Which achievement are you most proud of?**  
That the work created experiences people remember while also producing serious research around them.

**What evidence already demonstrates value?**  
Peer-reviewed research, participant testimony, academic partnerships, working solo and group experiences and a committed network around the work.

**What change should aNUma create over 3–5 years?**  
Make numadelic practice easier to access, safer to enter and possible to continue through trusted people and different formats.

**How should that change be measured?**  
Completed practices, return and referral, repeat cohorts, facilitator activity, paid renewals, participant-reported outcomes and safety data.

**What claim should the brand never make?**  
That aNUma guarantees transformation, connection, healing or a psychedelic-equivalent experience.

## 11 / Future

**What do people choose instead?**  
Meditation apps, retreats, therapy, breathwork, spiritual communities, wellness VR, psychedelic experiences—or nothing.

**What can aNUma credibly own?**  
The combination of immersive practice, weakly representational design, participant agency, relational experience and scientific inquiry.

**Which contradiction is a strength?**  
We are rigorous and mysterious, technological and embodied, individual and relational.

**Where is VR essential, and where might another medium work better?**  
VR is strongest for immersion, altered embodiment and remote co-presence. Mobile, web, lighter XR, wearables, installations and retreats may work better for access, preparation, continuity and shared physical settings.

**Most damaging identity risk**  
Remaining abstract and hard to explain.

**What would make 2027 successful?**  
aNUma has a clear paid offer, a refined solo journey, recurring facilitated practices and a companion layer that helps people prepare, integrate and return.

**Anything else?**  
The identity should launch with a working doorway, not ahead of one. The next release should prove a complete journey before expanding the ecosystem.

Clear pathway selection; Beautiful solo onboarding; Preparation and integration prompts; Safety and consent language.
